Lexi Arlen

Modeling sea ice dynamics across scales.

I am a PhD candidate in Earth System Science at Stanford University, in the Polar Ocean Dynamics Group led by Prof. Earle Wilson. I develop bonded discrete element models for process-based studies of sea ice dynamics, and I am broadly interested in how small-scale dynamics shape large-scale climate trends.

Research

Sea ice mediates ocean heat uptake, buffers ice shelves from ocean waves, and hosts the primary producers at the base of the polar food web — yet climate models still struggle to reproduce its trends, in part because they treat it as a continuous medium. I build discrete element models that resolve fracturing and floe–floe interaction directly, so the subgrid-scale physics can be parameterized rather than assumed.
