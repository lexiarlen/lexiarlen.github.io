Fieldwork

Two campaigns: a sea ice and wave programme in the Chukchi Sea, and a nodal seismic experiment in the Oregon coastal range.

Tale of Three Systems Cruise — Chukchi Sea, 2023

During the summer of 2023 we spent 45 days aboard the R/V Sikuliaq in the Chukchi Sea. We collected over 4,000 wave spectra alongside the science team for the Tale of Three Systems (ToTS) Cruise, which studied the evolution of under-ice phytoplankton blooms.

What I did

Floe size distribution from drone imagery

Conducted drone surveys to quantify the evolution of the floe size distribution over the melt season, and compiled the images into orthomosaics with Pix4D. Due to abnormally warm temperatures and calm winds, low fog often prevented flying.

Wave attenuation in sea ice

Deployed Sofar Ocean wave buoys to study ocean wave attenuation in ice, covering deployment design, field execution, and post-campaign analysis. We opportunistically tethered the buoys to the free-drifting sediment traps to simplify logistics, which allowed more deployments and retrievals. An anomalously calm summer hampered wave activity, so wave energy decayed rapidly below the instrument noise threshold on entering the ice.

Field operations

Helped deploy and recover sediment traps to quantify sediment fluxes in the water column, worked the multicorer to sample nutrients from the seafloor, and collected water from the conductivity, temperature, and depth (CTD) sensor.

Cascadia2021 Seismic Node Experiment — Oregon, 2021

During undergrad I participated in a seismological field campaign in Oregon, where we deployed and recovered over 800 nodal seismometers in the coastal mountains. A ship with an air gun array sailed the coast, generating sound waves that the seismometers detected, to image the structure of the subduction zone and better understand the megathrust earthquake hazard. I also managed the campaign blog.
